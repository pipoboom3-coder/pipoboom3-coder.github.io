Pipo & Boom / Пипо и Бум

Pipo & Boom is a YouTube channel of short sing-along song cartoons for children aged 1 to 4, in Russian and English. Pipo the little penguin and Boom the crane truck sing simple songs and play with their friends.

Pipo & Boom Uploader

The Pipo & Boom Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. After the owner has watched and approved a finished episode, the tool uploads that video, its title, description, tags and thumbnail to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, marked as made for kids. It does not access any other channel, does not read viewers' data and is not offered to other users.
